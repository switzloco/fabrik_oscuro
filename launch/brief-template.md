# Brief template

Paste a filled-in copy of this into the room, followed by the full specification. Everything specific to the project goes here, not in the mandates.

```text
Goal:
  <one or two sentences on what must exist when the work is done>

Specification:
  <paste the full written specification below this brief, or point to where it is in the repo>

Milestones:
  <the ordered milestones, and what each one's deliverable must contain>

Constraints:
  <runtime, language, resource limits, forbidden dependencies, anything fixed in advance>

Done state:
  <how the human will judge that a milestone or the whole task is complete>

Escalation:
  Ask me only when the specification does not settle a product decision.
  Offer one recommended default, not a menu of options.
```
