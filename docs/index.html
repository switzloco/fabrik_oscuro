What we learned

We ran out of time and quota after three stages: all three Codex seats hit their usage limit right after Stage 4 was dispatched. Provider routing, quota limits, and context control consumed time that should have gone into a cleaner autonomous run and later stages. The presentation records what we would change.

Supervised practice run: human execution approvals, host debugging, and UI checks were involved. No claim of full autonomy or Stage 4 completion. Earlier paid provider experimentation is separate from the final no-additional-spending practice.
